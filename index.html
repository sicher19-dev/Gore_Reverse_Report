The Story

The leakiest process night in a month — and the box score hid most of it. Net run value +0.66, the first positive since Seattle, wearing a winning 3-ER line. Eight hits, four for extra bases, all four off the fastball, three of those from the heart of the zone at 98.9 to 107.3 EV. The defense and the win column were kind. The ledger isn't supposed to be.

One pitch cost two runs, and it's the oldest sentence in these reports. The curve discipline was actually the best of the season — 12% zone to non-licensed hitters — and the single exception caught bottom-middle to Henry Bolte, who whiffs 57% on curves that finish BELOW the zone. 102.1 EV, over the wall, eighth straight start with a zone-curve receipt. The margin between his 57% whiff and his home run was about ten inches of depth. The rule has never been "mostly."

The wins were real and hard-earned: zero cutters, perfect sinker discipline, and the ladder printed all night. The first genuine zero-cutter start after four reports of asking. Not one sinker to Muncy, Heim or Bolte — the .817 landmine never detonated. And four of the five strikeouts came on elevated fastballs exactly as drawn: Muncy twice into his 53% hole, White, Serven. The vertical plan works even on a diminished heater.

The honest headline is the velocity. 94.6 — under the band for the first time in this stretch — on a fifth start in 23 days, two of them moved up a day. When the stuff dips and the addresses drift in the same start (three middle-middle fastballs doubled, a changeup at the letters), that's not two problems. It's one problem wearing two outfits. The next-start watch has a new number one, and it isn't a pitch.

Small graces, honestly counted. Three of the eight hits were sub-82 EV placement flares (.823, .934, .383 xBA) — noise, not process. Two 100+ EV outs were run down. And the A's sat both marquee matchups (Wilson, Soderstrom), so the scout's biggest plans go back on the shelf unused. Sometimes the night is just a grind. The ledger says grind, learn, and get the extra day of rest.
